How the agent works

  1. Muse Spark watches each video: camera type, edits, on-screen clock, when the fireball shows, and landmarks a map could locate.
  2. SAM 3.1 follows the fireball frame by frame from the phrase "bright light".
  3. Each camera is oriented against OpenStreetMap landmarks and street lines, so every pixel becomes a direction in the sky.
  4. One camera's lines of sight form a plane; two planes from different sides cross in the trajectory. Speed comes from each video's own frame times.
  5. Muse plans every step and writes the report. Tools return measured numbers, and geometry rules override the model.

Built during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon with Muse Spark and SAM 3.1. Witness videos belong to their authors and are not redistributed here: only directions, times and camera sites rounded to about 100 m.
